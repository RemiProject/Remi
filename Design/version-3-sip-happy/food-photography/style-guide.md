# Remi v3 food photography

Premium modern editorial food photography, bright soft natural side light, warm ivory limestone background, restrained Pinterest-style composition, authentic appetizing textures, subtle shadows, true colors, no text, no watermark, no logo, no people. One subject per image, no collage. Square composition with centered crop-safe subject and generous breathing room. High resolution, crisp food detail, no artificial plastic sheen.

Ingredients: standalone still life, accurately distinguish raw/cooked, flour/grain and varieties. Recipes: finished dish, faithful to its description and ingredient list. Never use an ingredient collage as a recipe photo.

Web masters: minimum 1200 pixels on the short edge; reject upscaled, blurred, cluttered, visibly watermarked, culturally incorrect or mismatched subjects. AI masters: request 1536 square or higher where supported; preserve native output (minimum 1024 square) without upscaling; mark synthetic provenance. Export app JPEG/WebP derivatives only after visual review. Keep masters and licenses. Pinterest is an aesthetic reference, not an image licensing source.
