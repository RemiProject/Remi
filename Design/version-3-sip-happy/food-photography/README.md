# Food photography (version 3)

Square photos of every catalogue recipe (the finished dish) and of 474 of the 800 catalogue ingredients. Warm ivory
limestone background, soft side light, one subject per image, no text. The direction is in [`style-guide.md`](style-guide.md).

| | Recipes | Ingredients |
|---|---|---|
| In the catalogue | 400 | 800 |
| Reviewed photo in the app | 400 | 474 |
| Still to generate | 0 | 326 (they keep the plain row without a photo) |

**Provenance.** 873 photos are AI-generated illustrations, marked as such in the app (More → photo note). One, garlic, is
a photograph: "Garlic bulbs and cloves" by Ivar Leidus, CC BY-SA 4.0, from Wikimedia Commons ([`CREDITS.md`](CREDITS.md)).
The app's cropped and resized copy is shared under the same licence.

**Files here.** [`image-map.json`](image-map.json) is the handoff's index: every catalogue entry, its status and its
master file. `contact-sheet-sample.jpg` is one of the review sheets. The full-resolution masters (1254 px, about 520 MB)
stay in the handoff folder, outside git.

**In the app.**
- `mobile-app/assets/food/recipes/*.webp`: 800 px, quality 72, about 66 KB each.
- `mobile-app/assets/food/ingredients/*.webp`: 256 px, quality 75, about 6 KB each.
- About 30 MB in all.
- `mobile-app/src/data/food-images.ts` maps names to files.
- Both are generated by `python3 mobile-app/scripts/import-food-images.py <handoff folder>`. The script:
  - checks each master's SHA-256 against the manifest;
  - takes only reviewed images;
  - centre-crops each one to a square and never upscales.

Re-run the script when the remaining ingredients arrive.

**Matching.**
- **Recipes:** `recipe_key` maps to the catalogue name through `recipes.csv`, and the app looks up the lower-cased name. The backend importer upserts global recipes by `lower(name)`, so the name is the stable link the app can see.
- **Ingredients:** the exact `foods.csv` name, its Arabic name and its listed aliases. When an alias of one food is another food's name, the name wins.
