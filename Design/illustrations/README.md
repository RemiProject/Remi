# Remi explainer illustrations

Three hand-drawn explainer illustrations made on 31 October 2026, in the "Xiaohei" style: a small black character on
white, with sparse red, orange and blue notes. They explain how Remi works and are not tied to one app version.
None of the app screens use them yet; they suit onboarding, empty states, the store listing and presentations.

| File | Shows |
|---|---|
| `01-the-fridge-remembers` | Adding food to the freezer, fridge and pantry. Every item gets a date ("12 foods, all remembered") |
| `02-one-pot-four-bowls` | One pot of chicken and rice split into four bowls, with nutrition counted per person (one serving each) |
| `03-phone-asks-kitchen-answers` | How the app talks to the backend. You log in first, then the phone sends a GraphQL request, the token is checked, and the answer comes back from the backend (851 foods, 400 recipes, your kitchen) |

Each comes as an editable `.svg` and a 1600×900 `.png`. The originals stay in the repo-root `assets/remy-app-illustrations/`,
which is not committed.
