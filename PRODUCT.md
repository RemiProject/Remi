# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
adaptive

## Users
Household members managing ingredients, meals and shopping.

## Product Purpose
Remi brings household inventory, recipes, cooking, planning, shopping and nutrition into one app.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing Expo SDK 58 React Native app and NestJS GraphQL backend. Preserve English/Arabic support, authentication and household isolation. The independent build must preserve the original source and data. External provider credentials are not assumed.

## Brand Commitments
Version 3 "Sip Happy" direction (user-approved, replaces Living Cookbook):
- **Palette:** lemon yellow #F8E45C as base, cobalt blue #1734BE for action, hot pink #ED2B8C as a sparing pop.
- **Type:** bold DM Sans headings.
- **Components:** white rounded photo cards and pill controls.
- **Cooking and planning:** Mob-style planning and a full-screen cook mode.
- **Native controls:** compact and familiar.
