# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
adaptive

## Users
Household members managing ingredients, meals and shopping.

## Product Purpose
Remi brings household inventory, recipes, cooking, planning, shopping and nutrition into one app.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing Expo SDK 58 React Native app and NestJS GraphQL backend. Preserve English/Arabic support, authentication and household isolation. The independent build must preserve the original source and data. External provider credentials are not assumed.

## Brand Commitments
User-approved Living Cookbook direction: warm subtle paper, expressive editorial serif, rich tomato red/butter yellow/leafy green, food imagery and fluid purposeful motion. Compact familiar native controls.

## Evidence on Hand
Original source snapshot, existing API schema and tests, approved visual boards in ../remi-ui-concepts.
